LootTracker Forever

Upload your loot data

Send what your LootTracker Forever addon recorded, or your Wowhead Looter file if that's what you run. Lokiy checks every upload before it shows up on the site.

  1. Log out or type /reload in game: WoW only saves addon data then.
  2. Find your file: World of Warcraft\_forever_\WTF\Account\<ACCOUNT>\SavedVariables\LootTrackerForever.lua (or +Wowhead_Looter.lua in the same folder: only its loot is used, and it has less detail than our addon)
  3. Enter your upload code (from Lokiy), choose the file and press Upload.
  4. Once it says your file is safe on the server, you can clear your data in game with /ltf reset confirm so the file stays small. Your next upload then only holds what's new.

Only the file you choose is sent. It's checked as data and never run. It holds your characters' names, what you looted, and the names of players you grouped with.
