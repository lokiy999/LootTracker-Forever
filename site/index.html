Rates are per kill, from players' uploads. A kill counts when its corpse was looted, or when a player on their own killed a mob of theirs and it left nothing. Kills in a group count once looted, because the rest may have gone to someone else, so rates can read a little high. In dungeons the game hides which mob died, so there rates are per looted corpse. Gathering, disenchanting and crafting have their own tabs and never count as drops. Low counts mean little: check the numbers next to each rate.
